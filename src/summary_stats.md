# Summary Statistics

## Gini Mean Difference

The Gini Mean Difference (GMD) answers the question: how far apart are these values, pair by pair?

More formally, given a collection of values $x_1, x_2, ..., x_n$, the GMD is:

$$\frac{1}{{n \choose 2}} \sum_{i < j} |x_i - x_j|$$

In other words, compute the mean of all pairwise differences.

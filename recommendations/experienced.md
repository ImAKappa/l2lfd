# Recommendations - For Experienced Learners

This guide recommends resources for those who have experience in statistics and machine learning, but who wish to solidify their fundamentals, find new perspectives of their knowledge, and seriously elevate their skills.

I expect you to have either studied these topics in a college or university degree, for work, or on your own time for at least several months to a few years.

The recommendations here can be a bit difficult (or at least novel compared to your university studies), but are very high-quality. They are very much worth your time.

## Linear Algebra

📘 [Linear Algebra Done Right (4e) - Sheldon Axler](https://linear.axler.net/LADR4e.pdf)

The book provides clear and succint explanations of fundamental linear algebra concepts.
Many examples of proofs. The book introduces concepts not in the typical order in a linear algebra course (such as starting with fields and inner product spaces before discussing determinants), but I think linear algebra is indeed done right in Axler's work.

## Statistics

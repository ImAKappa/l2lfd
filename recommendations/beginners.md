# Recommendations - For Beginners

This guide recommends resources for those who have little to no experience in statistics and machine learning, but who wish to learn.

There are too many good resources out there, but not enough time. So, this resource list prioritizes gentle introductions. Additionally, all resources here must be freely available online.

In each section, I offer my single, best beginner:

- 📽️ Course/video playlist
- 📘 Book
- 🧪 Scientific publication
- 🌐 Other online resource (Blog post, game, infographic, etc.)
